# Crisis Preparedness Checklist

Go through this checklist **now** while you still have internet and power. Tick off each item as you complete it.

## Tier 1: Digital Preparedness

- [ ] Download Mukto Mesh from GitHub Releases and save the installer on your laptop and a USB drive
- [ ] Test that `start.bat` runs and the dashboard loads at `http://localhost:3000`
- [ ] Save emergency contacts from the Info page into your phone
- [ ] Save offline maps of your city on your phone (Google Maps offline or Organic Maps)
- [ ] Back up important documents (NID, passport, medical records) as photos in your phone
- [ ] Print the crisis checklist and emergency contacts from this app — keep the paper copy in your bag
- [ ] Install a mesh messenger app on your phone (Briar, Bridgefy, or BitChat) as backup
- [ ] Set up a VPN on your phone and laptop before you need it
- [ ] Share Mukto Mesh with at least 3 neighbours and agree on who will run the node
- [ ] Charge all devices fully. Keep power banks charged.

## Tier 2: Physical 72-Hour Kit

### Water and Food
- [ ] 3 litres of drinking water per person per day (minimum)
- [ ] 3 days of non-perishable food (dry biscuits, canned food, dried fruits, nuts)
- [ ] Water purification tablets or a portable filter

### First Aid and Health
- [ ] First aid kit (see First Aid section for contents)
- [ ] 7-day supply of essential medications (blood pressure, diabetes, asthma, etc.)
- [ ] Pain relievers, antacids, oral rehydration salts
- [ ] Sanitary pads and hygiene products
- [ ] Masks (N95 or surgical)

### Tools and Supplies
- [ ] Flashlight with extra batteries
- [ ] Power bank (fully charged) with charging cables
- [ ] Cash in small denominations (enough for 7 days)
- [ ] Matches or lighter in a waterproof container
- [ ] Multi-tool or Swiss knife
- [ ] Whistle (to signal for help)
- [ ] Plastic bags (for waste, waterproofing)
- [ ] Notebook and pen
- [ ] Battery-powered or hand-crank radio

## Tier 3: Communication Plan

- [ ] Agree on a primary meeting point within walking distance
- [ ] Agree on a secondary meeting point outside your neighbourhood
- [ ] Identify one out-of-area contact everyone can call when internet returns
- [ ] Share phone numbers with at least 2 neighbours
- [ ] Agree on who will run the Mukto Mesh node
- [ ] Agree on emergency signals (e.g., 3 knocks = safe, 1 knock = danger)

## Tier 4: During a Shutdown

- [ ] Stay calm. Do not panic.
- [ ] Start the Mukto Mesh node and share the hotspot
- [ ] Post the node address and hotspot name in common areas
- [ ] Check in on the app so others know you are safe
- [ ] Conserve phone battery — turn off background data, reduce brightness
- [ ] Verify all information before sharing. If unsure, ask in the chat
- [ ] Check on elderly or disabled neighbours who may not be on the app
- [ ] Keep children informed without causing fear — give them simple tasks
- [ ] Do not share your location on public social media

## Tier 5: After the Crisis

- [ ] Restock your emergency kit
- [ ] Update your emergency contacts list
- [ ] Update the Mukto Mesh app to the latest version
- [ ] Review what worked and what didn't. Update your plan
- [ ] Help someone else prepare
