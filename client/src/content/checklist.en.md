# Crisis Preparedness Checklist

## Before a Shutdown — Do This Now
- [ ] Download Mukto Mesh on your laptop
- [ ] Save emergency contact numbers offline
- [ ] Know your local node operator or agree to be one
- [ ] Download offline maps of your area
- [ ] Agree on a physical meeting point with family
- [ ] Charge all devices and keep a power bank ready
- [ ] Keep cash on hand — digital payments fail in shutdowns
- [ ] Store 72 hours of water (3 litres per person per day)
- [ ] Store 72 hours of non-perishable food
- [ ] Keep a basic first aid kit at home
- [ ] Know the location of your nearest hospital

## During a Shutdown
- [ ] Start a Mukto Mesh node immediately if you have a laptop
- [ ] Share your local IP with neighbours
- [ ] Post your status on the noticeboard
- [ ] Register for check-in if you are in a dangerous area
- [ ] Do not spread unverified information
- [ ] Check the knowledge base before acting on rumours
