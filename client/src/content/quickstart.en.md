# Quick Start Guide

This guide explains every feature in Mukto Mesh so you can use the app immediately during a crisis.

## Connecting to a Node

1. Turn on your phone's WiFi.
2. Look for the hotspot named after the node (ask your node operator for the name).
3. Connect — no password is required unless the operator set one.
4. Open your phone browser and go to the address shown by the node operator (usually `http://192.168.x.x:3000`).
5. The app loads. You're in.

## Setting Your Display Name

The first time you open the app, a popup will ask for your display name. This is how other people in your community will see you in chat and on the noticeboard.

- Choose a name people will recognise.
- You only need to set it once — it's saved in your browser.
- You can change it anytime from the profile icon.

## Navigation

The app has seven sections, accessible from the bottom tab bar:

| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Live crisis metrics: connected users, check-ins, posts, missing persons, news, map pins. Quick actions to jump anywhere. |
| **Chat** | Real-time messaging over LAN in four channels: General, Emergency, Coordination, Medical. |
| **Board** | Community noticeboard. Read pinned alerts and official posts from your node admin. |
| **Info** | This knowledge base — crisis information in Bangla and English. Works fully offline. |
| **People** | Missing person registry. Report someone missing or search for loved ones. |
| **Map** | Offline Bangladesh map with community pins (shelters, danger zones, aid points). |
| **News** | Cached news from trusted Bangladeshi sources, available for offline reading. |

## Language

Tap the language toggle (EN / BN) at the top of any page to switch between English and Bangla. All content, menus, and the knowledge base will switch immediately.

## No account needed

There is no sign-up, no password, no phone number required. Just connect and use it.
