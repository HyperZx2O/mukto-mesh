# About Mukto Mesh

**Mukto Mesh** is a lightweight, offline-first community hub that turns any laptop into a local crisis coordination node. No internet required. No technical expertise needed.

## Why this app exists

In July 2024, the Bangladesh government shut down the internet for days. People couldn't find missing family members. Volunteers couldn't reach the injured. Nobody knew what was true — rumours filled the gap that reliable information left behind.

That wasn't bad luck. It was a deliberate cut, and ordinary people had nothing to fall back on.

VPNs need foresight. Satellite internet needs money. Most people in a crisis have neither. **Mukto Mesh** was built for this specific context — in Bangla and English, for Bangladesh, so any person with a laptop can keep their neighbourhood connected when the internet dies.

## How it works

1. One person downloads Mukto Mesh on their laptop (while the internet is still on).
2. They run it — one command or double-click `start.bat`.
3. Anyone nearby connects to the laptop's WiFi hotspot and opens any browser.
4. The full app loads: chat, noticeboard, knowledge base, missing persons registry, check-in system, offline maps, and cached news.
5. Everything works without internet. The laptop is the server.

## Privacy

- Mukto Mesh does **not** track your location, collect personal data, or send anything to a third party.
- All data stays on the local node's SQLite database.
- When internet is available, optional remote sync can share missing person reports and map pins with a central server.
- You control what you share.
