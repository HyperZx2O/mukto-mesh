# First Aid

## Tear Gas Exposure
- Move upwind and to higher ground immediately.
- Do not rub your eyes — this makes it worse.
- Flush eyes with clean water for 10–15 minutes.
- Remove and bag contaminated clothing.
- Wash skin with soap and water.

## Crowd Crush
- Keep your arms up near your chest to protect your lungs.
- Move diagonally to the edge of the crowd.
- If you fall, curl into a ball and protect your head.
- Do not fight the crowd — move with it and angle toward the edge.

## Gunshot Wound (Basic)
- Apply firm, direct pressure to the wound immediately.
- Do not remove the object if something is embedded.
- Keep the person still and warm.
- Get to a hospital as fast as possible — this is a medical emergency.

## Burns
- Cool the burn with running water for 20 minutes.
- Do not use ice, butter, or toothpaste.
- Cover loosely with a clean cloth.
- Seek medical help for burns larger than your palm.

## Unconscious Person
- Check for breathing. If not breathing and you know CPR, begin immediately.
- Place in recovery position if breathing: on their side, head tilted back.
- Do not leave them alone.
- Get help immediately.
