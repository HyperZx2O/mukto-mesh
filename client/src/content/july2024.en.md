# July 2024 — What We Learned

## What Happened
From July 1 to August 5, 2024, a mass uprising against the quota reform system transformed into a nationwide revolution that ended 15 years of authoritarian rule. The government imposed a nationwide internet shutdown lasting several days — cutting off 170 million people at the moment they needed each other most.

## What Worked
- **Jogajog** — students built decentralised communication networks using Bluetooth, offline messaging apps, and physical word of mouth.
- **Neighbourhood coordination** — local communities organised without central direction.
- **Pre-downloaded information** — people who had saved emergency contacts and legal rights guides offline were better prepared.
- **Trusted physical meeting points** — agreed locations meant people could find each other without phones.

## What Failed
- Reliance on a single platform (Facebook) which was blocked.
- No pre-established fallback communication plan.
- No offline maps for locating shelters or hospitals.
- Missing person coordination was chaotic — no central registry.
