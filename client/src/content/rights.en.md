# Your Rights During a Crisis

## If You Are Stopped by Police
- You have the right to know why you are being stopped.
- You are not required to answer questions beyond identifying yourself.
- You have the right to remain silent. State clearly: "I am exercising my right to silence."
- Do not resist physically even if you believe the stop is unlawful.

## If You Are Arrested
- Police must inform you of the reason for arrest.
- You have the right to contact a lawyer immediately.
- You have the right to inform a family member of your arrest.
- You cannot be detained for more than 24 hours without being presented before a magistrate.

## Right to Peaceful Assembly
- The right to peaceful assembly is protected under Article 37 of the Bangladesh Constitution.
- Authorities must have lawful grounds to disperse a peaceful gathering.
