## Community Networks

During a shutdown, these national networks may be unreachable by phone. Always have a backup plan: identify a local community health worker, a nearby pharmacy, a religious leader, or a neighbourhood watch contact.

> **Tip:** Save at least two of these numbers in your phone now. Write them on paper and keep it in your emergency kit.
